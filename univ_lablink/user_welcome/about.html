What is this system?

A web-based borrowing and management portal for laboratory items and equipment at Lyceum of the Philippines University Cavite. Requests, approvals, returns, and inventory records come together in one place.

One simple cycle

Find available equipment, submit an online request, collect approved items, and return them to the laboratory custodian for inspection.

Check availability

See current stock before you make a request.

Request and approval

Submit one online form for laboratory staff review.

Track deadlines

Follow request status and due dates from your dashboard.

Terms and conditions

  1. Use your own active account and keep your student details current.
  2. Borrowing requests require staff approval and are subject to available stock.
  3. Return items by the stated deadline; request extensions before they are due.
  4. Handle equipment carefully. Loss or damage may require repair or replacement.
  5. Follow laboratory safety rules and all custodian instructions.
